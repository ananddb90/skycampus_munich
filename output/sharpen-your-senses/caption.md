Did you know every cell in your body can see, hear and feel?

Not just your eyes or ears — Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar teaches that every single cell carries that same potential, which is why people who lose one sense so often find the others growing sharper. You don't actually have to lose a sense to sharpen it, though. You just have to get quiet enough to notice what's already there.

Just sit in silence, and notice what's already there.
