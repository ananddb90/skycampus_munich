Why does loyalty feel so rare these days?

Because disloyalty isn't usually malice — it's opportunism wearing a nicer name. A divided mind can't stay loyal to anything, including itself. Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar describes loyalty as starting with a "yes-mind": quiet, whole, and unafraid to commit, versus the agitated "no-mind" that doubts everything including itself. Loyalty isn't about who you owe it to. It's about how undivided you're willing to be.

That's where loyalty begins.
