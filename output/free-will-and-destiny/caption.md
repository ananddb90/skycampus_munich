What if you're using free will and destiny backwards?

Most of us treat the past like it was fully our choice - so we carry regret over things we did or didn't do. And we treat the future like it's already decided - so we stop trying, half-expecting things to go a certain way regardless of what we do.

Flip it. Let the past be destiny - fixed, done, nothing left to negotiate. Let the future be free will - open, unwritten, yours to shape.

The present moment is where the divine actually lives.
