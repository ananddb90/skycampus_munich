Why does uncertainty feel so unbearable?

Because most of us place our certainty in the wrong place — in a world that was never meant to be certain, that's always changing. Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar points to what doesn't change: the one who's watching it all change. You. Rely on that, and the world's uncertainty stops being a threat. Being certain that everything is uncertain — that's actually freedom, not fear.

Let the ground move. You don't have to.
