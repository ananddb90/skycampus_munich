Is your memory helping you, or holding you hostage?

Remembering who you really are - underneath the roles, the résumé, the noise - is a gift. It's the thing that brings you back to yourself on a hard day. But remembering every old hurt, every old high, replaying it on a loop? That's a cage. Same memory, two very different jobs.

The point was never to forget. It's to use memory to remember yourself, not to relive your history.
