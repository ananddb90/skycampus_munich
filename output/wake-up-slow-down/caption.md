Why does rushing rob you of the very thing you're rushing toward?

In a hurry, you can't actually see anything clearly - not the people around you, not the moment itself, not even what you're chasing. The rush isn't really about time. It's a feeling of never having enough of it, and that feeling doesn't go away just because you move faster.

Slowing down isn't laziness. It's just being awake. And once you're actually awake, you can't help but move well.
