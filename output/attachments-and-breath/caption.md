What if your attachments are why you can't breathe easy?

Every attachment - to an outcome, a person, an idea of how things should go - quietly speeds up your breath and steals your peace, usually before you even notice it happening. You don't have to fight it or force yourself to care less. Just notice the feverishness, and soften around it.

Non-attachment to the small stuff isn't detachment from life. It's actually what makes you charming - easy to be around, easy to trust.

Hold loosely. Breathe easy.
