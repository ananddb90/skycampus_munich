Why are confident people rarely humble, and humble people rarely confident?

Try this: zoom out far enough and your whole life looks tiny against the scale of time and space. That's humility. Now zoom back in - you're the only you that will ever exist, doing something no one else can do exactly the way you do it. That's confidence.

Most people pick one distance and live there. But hold both at once, and something rare happens: you stop needing to prove anything, and you stop needing to shrink either.

Confidence and humility were never opposites.
