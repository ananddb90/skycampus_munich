What if your teacher never wanted followers?

A guru who's ahead of you can only lead you in circles. Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar put it differently: the real one stands behind you, pushing you forward — not to be followed, but to free you from ever needing to follow. You've spent long enough being the sheep. It's time to move like a lion, and leave even the teacher behind.

No one to follow. Only yourself to become.
