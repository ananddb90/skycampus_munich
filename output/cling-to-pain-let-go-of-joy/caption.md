Why do we cling to pain but let go of joy so easily?

We hold onto hurt like it's protecting us from more of it. Meanwhile, joy barely gets a chance to land before we're second-guessing it, waiting for it to end. But joy needs space to breathe, and a tight grip leaves none.

Loosen the hand a little. Not because the pain doesn't matter, but because holding on this tight is exhausting - and the little things stop shaking you the moment you do.

Growth is just what happens when you finally let go.
