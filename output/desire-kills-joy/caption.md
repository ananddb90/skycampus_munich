What if the thing you're chasing is what's keeping happiness away?

Desire kills joy — and yet, as Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar points out, every desire is aimed at joy. No one has ever wished for unhappiness, not once, not ever. So when joy quietly disappears from your life, look closer: desire is usually hiding right there, disguised as ambition. The one who masters desire is called a conqueror — not because they got everything, but because they stopped needing to.

Not because they got everything. Because they stopped needing to.
