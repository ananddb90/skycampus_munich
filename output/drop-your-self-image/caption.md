What stands between you and me is your self-image.

Sri Sri Ravi Shankar puts it simply: whether you think well of yourself or poorly of yourself, that image is still standing between you and everyone else. Thinking too highly of yourself quietly breeds judgment of others. Thinking too little breeds the same, aimed inward first.

Drop the self-image. What's left is just you, unguarded.
