What if silence isn't the absence of anything?

We tend to think of silence as a gap - the pause between words, the quiet after noise. But real silence is something you carry, not something you wait for. It's prayer inside your own breath, love that asks for no reason, compassion with no agenda attached.

You don't need a quiet room to find it. It's already available in your next inhale.

Silence isn't empty. It's full.
