Ever notice how judgment sneaks in even when you're trying not to judge?

Here's the thing: you can't actually escape it. Judging is how you choose what's good for you - which apple to buy, who to trust, what deserves your time. The trouble isn't the judgment itself. It's when a judgment hardens into a permanent verdict about a person who's still changing, same as you are.

Let it move through you like a breeze, not settle like a stone.

And since you can't stop judging altogether, at least judge your company well.
