Why do you only doubt the good things in your life?

Notice it: you never doubt your sadness, your anger, your bad days. You just accept them as fact. But the second something good shows up - love, a compliment, a win - doubt sneaks in and asks "is this real?"

Doubt is just standing with one foot on each shore, neither here nor there. It's exhausting because it's unstable. The fix isn't more certainty about everything. It's picking a shore for the good stuff too, and standing on it.

Doubt the doubt. Trust what's good.
