Is boredom trying to tell you something?

Boredom isn't a problem to escape - it's just thought on repeat with no love left in it. Our instinct is to reach for a new distraction the moment it shows up. But the actual fix is the opposite: go deeper into the same thing you're bored of, instead of switching to something new.

Even love repeats itself in every letter, every gesture, every ordinary Tuesday - and it never gets old. Boredom only wins when you run from it.

Push through it and you meet yourself.
