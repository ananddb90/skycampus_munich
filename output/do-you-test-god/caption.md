Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar put it simply: God already knows your past, present and future, so there's nothing left to test. If He had to pass your test, He was never God to begin with. Same goes for people, plans, and yourself. Swipe through, and see where you're still testing instead of trusting.

That's the whole secret.
